SILK Homes: Rooted in Community, Dedicated to Hospitality

Historic, cozy homes in West Virginia and Ohio, where we cultivate gardens, practice sustainability, and offer a peaceful retreat for our guests and our crew.

Welcome to SILK Homes

We are more than just housing. We are a collection of historical homes where we practice sustainability, community, and peaceful living. We are looking for people who don't just want a job, but want to contribute to and live within this culture.
