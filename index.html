Grown from a Commune, Built on Community

The Founder's Vision

SILK Homes is inspired by its founder's upbringing on a 100-acre family commune, where ten houses shared resources, labor, and life. We carry that spirit forward by creating a network of homes built on mutual support and a love for the land.

That early life taught us a fundamental lesson: that sharing resources and skills doesn't diminish them, it multiplies them. SILK Homes is our way of bringing that communal spirit into the modern world.

Our Philosophy

We believe in peaceful living, self-sufficiency, and finding beauty in the historical. We thrift for materials, giving new life to old treasures. Every property has its own victory garden, providing fresh produce and a connection to the earth.

Why thrift? Because we believe our homes should have soul. Every piece of furniture, every repaired fixture, tells a story. It's a sustainable choice and a rejection of the disposable.

Our Victory Gardens are more than just a source of food. They are a classroom, a quiet retreat, and a tangible connection to the seasons and the land.

Our Homes

Our portfolio is a collection of character-rich townhomes, each on about a quarter-acre of land. We are proud stewards of four homes built in the 1800s, four from the 1950s, and several more modern dwellings. Each one is a canvas for our hospitality crew to maintain and cherish.

We don't just rent houses; we cultivate a community network, much like the one I grew up in.

Our Guiding Principles

🗝️

Hospitality is an Art of Welcome

For us, hospitality is not a transaction. It is the craft of creating a space where people feel genuinely welcome, safe, and at peace. It's in the clean rooms, the thoughtful details, and the warm communication. We are hosts in the truest sense of the word.

🏛️

Honor the History, Build the Future

Our homes have stood for decades, some for over a century. We see ourselves as their current stewards, not just owners. We honor their past by repairing with care, and we build their future by making them sustainable and resilient for the years to come.

🌱

Hands in the Soil, Roots in the Community

A home is more than its four walls. It extends to the land it sits on. Our victory gardens are a vital part of each property, providing food, beauty, and a connection to the earth. We encourage our crew and guests to enjoy the simple pleasure of a garden-fresh meal.

♻️

Resourcefulness is the New Luxury

We reject a disposable culture. We find luxury in a well-made, thrifted piece of furniture, in the skill of a repair, and in the creativity of using what we have. This approach is not only sustainable but fills our homes with soul and story.

🌙

Peaceful Living is Paramount

Our homes are designed to be sanctuaries. They are quiet, calm spaces for rest and rejuvenation, especially for our guests who are often hardworking medical professionals. We cultivate a culture of mutual respect for peace and privacy among all our guests and tenants.

Project Spotlight: Breathing New Life into the Ravenswood Porches

July 15, 2025

This past month, we focused on the four 1890s cottages in Ravenswood. The front porches were showing their age, so we spent our time carefully replacing worn boards, sanding down the old paint, and giving them a fresh coat of slate blue. It's a small change that makes a world of difference in welcoming our guests home. A perfect example of honoring the history while building the future.
