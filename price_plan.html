Choose your pace

Ways to stay, made clear.

Every SILK home is furnished for real life: private space, shared amenities, reliable connectivity, and a calm setting in which to settle. The best fit depends on your dates, the home, and how you want to participate.

01 A few nights to a few weeks

Short stay

A furnished home base for restorative nights, a quiet working week, or a change of rhythm.

Your quote Tailored to your dates Home, season, length, and availability all matter.

What to expect

  • Private bedroom and shared common spaces
  • Flexible kitchen and dining space
  • Room for your own routines and practices
  • Character, quiet, and a place to settle
Ask about availability
02 For friends, families, and small teams

Shared home stay

Bring a small group into a furnished home with room for shared meals, focused work, and meaningful downtime.

Your quote Shaped around the group We’ll confirm rooms, dates, house fit, and support together.

What to expect

  • Room for a small group or crew
  • Shared spaces for meals and conversation
  • Reliable Wi-Fi, kitchen, and living areas
  • Clear coordination before arrival

03 For a steadier season of life

Mid-term home base

For traveling professionals, remote workers, and anyone who wants the steadiness of a home rather than a hotel room.

Your quote Built around the season Longer stays are discussed with the home and schedule in view.

What to expect

  • Private room with furnished common space
  • Kitchen arranged for everyday living
  • Nearby towns, rivers, parks, and local life
  • Linens and practical home essentials
