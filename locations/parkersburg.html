Parkersburg, West Virginia

A practical river-city network of furnished rooms, working kitchens, and town access.

Urban Homesteading

Parkersburg is a larger Mid-Ohio Valley town: useful for work, services, river walks, and the ordinary errands that make a longer stay feel possible.

The homes are not interchangeable, and the public archive is being curated property by property. Current imagery here focuses on common rooms, kitchens, and the practical details that make furnished living feel grounded.

Why Parkersburg Works

🏥 Healthcare Hub

Home to Camden Clark Medical Center and numerous healthcare facilities, making it ideal for traveling medical professionals.

🛍️ Urban Conveniences

Full grocery stores, restaurants, shopping, and services within easy reach of all properties.

🌳 Green Spaces

City Park, Southwood Park, and the Ohio River provide ample opportunities for outdoor recreation.

🎭 Cultural Scene

Smoot Theatre, Blennerhassett Museum, and a thriving arts district provide cultural enrichment.
