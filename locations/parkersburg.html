Parkersburg, West Virginia

Mid-century modern charm meets urban convenience in our collection of thoughtfully restored 1950s homes.

Urban Homesteading

Parkersburg offers the perfect blend of small-town charm and urban amenities. Our four carefully selected 1950s homes showcase post-war architectural optimism while providing modern comfort for today's travelers.

Located throughout Parkersburg's established neighborhoods, each property features its own productive victory garden and has been updated with sustainable systems while preserving the clean lines and functional design that make mid-century homes so appealing.

Our Parkersburg Properties

1950s Ranch Home - Modernist Style

The Modern Ranch

Built: 1954 • Style: Ranch • Guests: 4

Clean lines, large windows, and an open floor plan showcase the optimism of 1950s design. Features the largest victory garden in Parkersburg.

Cape Cod Style Home with Garden

The Cape Cod

Built: 1951 • Style: Cape Cod • Guests: 6

Traditional styling with modern efficiency. Popular with families and longer-term stays, featuring a productive herb and vegetable garden.

Additional properties coming online throughout 2025. Each one carefully selected for its architectural integrity and garden potential.

Why Parkersburg Works

🏥 Healthcare Hub

Home to Camden Clark Medical Center and numerous healthcare facilities, making it ideal for traveling medical professionals.

🛍️ Urban Conveniences

Full grocery stores, restaurants, shopping, and services within easy reach of all properties.

🌳 Green Spaces

City Park, Southwood Park, and the Ohio River provide ample opportunities for outdoor recreation.

🎭 Cultural Scene

Smoot Theatre, Blennerhassett Museum, and a thriving arts district provide cultural enrichment.
