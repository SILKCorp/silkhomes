Ravenswood, West Virginia

Four historic 1890s cottages nestled along the Ohio River, forming the heart of our community cluster.

Where History Meets Community

Ravenswood represents the crown jewel of our SILK Homes portfolio—four magnificent cottages built in the 1890s, each carefully restored to preserve their historic character while providing modern comfort for our guests.

These homes don't just sit side by side; they form a true community cluster where our live-in innkeeper oversees daily operations, guests naturally connect with one another, and our victory gardens create shared spaces for gathering and growing.

Our Ravenswood Properties

Historic Cottage #1 - "The Riverside"

The Riverside Cottage

Built: 1892 • Bedrooms: 2 • Guests: 4

The closest to the Ohio River, this cottage features original hardwood floors, a wraparound porch, and views of the water. The large front garden specializes in herbs and medicinal plants.

  • Original Victorian details preserved
  • Modern kitchen with vintage charm
  • Private herb garden
  • River views from front porch
Historic Cottage #2 - "The Garden House"

The Garden House

Built: 1889 • Bedrooms: 3 • Guests: 6

Our largest Ravenswood cottage, surrounded by the most productive victory garden in our entire portfolio. Perfect for longer stays and small groups.

  • Spacious layout with period details
  • Large eat-in kitchen
  • Extensive vegetable garden
  • Original stained glass windows
Historic Cottage #3 - "The Craftsman"

The Craftsman

Built: 1894 • Bedrooms: 2 • Guests: 4

Features beautiful original woodwork and built-in cabinets. This cottage showcases the finest craftsmanship of the era with modern sustainable updates.

  • Exceptional original woodwork
  • Built-in furniture and storage
  • Energy-efficient updates
  • Quiet reading nook
Historic Cottage #4 - "The Innkeeper's Residence"

The Innkeeper's Residence

Built: 1891 • Purpose: Crew Housing

Home to our live-in innkeeper, this cottage serves as the community hub. While not available for guest stays, it's the heart of our Ravenswood operations.

  • Private residence for innkeeper
  • Community gathering space
  • Garden tool and supply storage
  • Operations center for cluster

Life in the Ravenswood Cluster

What makes Ravenswood special isn't just the historic architecture—it's the community life that naturally develops when like-minded people share a beautiful space.

🌅 Morning Garden Tours

Our innkeeper leads informal walks through the gardens, sharing knowledge about the plants and invite guests to help with harvesting.

🍅 Shared Harvests

Guests are encouraged to use fresh produce from the gardens in their cooking, with recipe suggestions and preparation tips provided.

🔨 Restoration Projects

Interested guests can participate in ongoing restoration work, learning traditional building techniques while contributing to the homes' preservation.

🌊 River Access

The Ohio River provides opportunities for peaceful walks, fishing, and quiet contemplation just steps from your door.

Exploring Ravenswood

While our cottages provide a peaceful retreat, the town of Ravenswood offers charming local attractions and essential services.

Historic Downtown

Walkable main street with local shops, antique stores, and the famous Ravenswood Farmers Market (Saturdays).

Ohio River Trail

Miles of walking and biking paths along the river, perfect for morning jogs or evening strolls.

Local Eateries

Several family-owned restaurants serving everything from comfort food to farm-to-table cuisine.

Healthcare Access

Community hospital and medical facilities within 15 minutes, making it ideal for traveling medical professionals.

Arts & Culture

Local arts center with rotating exhibits, community theater, and seasonal festivals celebrating Appalachian culture.

Natural Beauty

Close to several state parks and nature preserves for hiking, kayaking, and wildlife observation.
