Marietta, Ohio

Ohio's first permanent settlement, where the Muskingum meets the Ohio River, offering rich history and vibrant community life.

Where History Lives

Marietta holds a special place in American history as Ohio's first permanent settlement, founded in 1788. Today, it combines that rich heritage with a thriving arts scene, excellent healthcare facilities, and a strong commitment to environmental stewardship.

Our Marietta properties reflect the town's character—thoughtfully restored homes that honor the past while embracing sustainable living for the future. With Sarah as our dedicated innkeeper, guests experience the warmth of genuine hospitality in a community that values both tradition and innovation.

The Marietta Experience

🏛️ Living History

Walk through America's oldest planned city, visit the Campus Martius Museum, and explore the historic Harmar Village.

🎨 Arts & Culture

Vibrant arts scene with galleries, theater, annual festivals, and the historic Lafayette Hotel hosting cultural events.

🚣 River Activities

Where the Muskingum meets the Ohio River—perfect for kayaking, fishing, and peaceful riverside walks.

🏥 Healthcare Excellence

Marietta Memorial Hospital and regional medical facilities make this ideal for traveling healthcare professionals.

🌿 Environmental Leadership

Strong commitment to sustainability with green initiatives, farmers markets, and environmental education programs.

🎓 Educational Hub

Home to Marietta College, bringing youthful energy and intellectual vibrancy to this historic community.
