Living Simply

Private space. Shared community. Peaceful coexistence. Know what to expect before you apply.

Let's Be Clear

SILK Homes is intentional living with healthy boundaries. We're selective about who joins us because fit matters for everyone's peace.

What We Are

  • Private bedrooms in shared homes
  • Peaceful coexistence with respectful neighbors
  • Shared kitchens, bathrooms, and common areas
  • A network of homes, not a single compound
  • Minimalist spaces designed for focus and calm
  • Smart home technology for modern convenience
  • A launching pad for future entrepreneurs
  • Part of the SILK YACHT ecosystem

What We're NOT

  • A commune where everyone does everything together
  • A party house or social scene
  • Short-term vacation rentals
  • A place to "figure things out"
  • Free housing with no expectations
  • A therapy program or support facility
  • Pet-friendly (no exceptions)
  • A place for substance use of any kind
This is NOT for everyone. That's the point.

We're building a community of capable, self-directed individuals who contribute more than they consume. If you need constant social interaction or supervision, this isn't the right fit.

How It Actually Works

Your Bedroom

Your private sanctuary. Furnished simply with quality basics. This is YOUR space. We don't enter without permission. You're responsible for keeping it clean and orderly.

Included
  • Bed with quality linens
  • Dresser and closet space
  • Desk or work surface
  • Good lighting
  • Climate control
Shared Spaces

Kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, and porches are shared. Clean up after yourself immediately. Leave spaces better than you found them. Respect quiet hours.

Expectations
  • Clean as you go - no exceptions
  • Label your food, respect others'
  • Quiet hours: 9 PM - 8 AM
  • Schedule shared resources fairly
  • Report maintenance issues promptly

Lifestyle Expectations

Minimalism

Declutter before you arrive. We provide the essentials. You bring only what you truly need. Less stuff means more peace, easier cleaning, and room for what matters.

Bring

Clothes for all seasons, personal care items, laptop/devices, a few meaningful personal items

Leave Behind

Excess furniture, storage boxes, collections, anything you haven't used in 6 months

Substance-Free

No alcohol. No recreational drugs. No smoking/vaping (including cannabis). This is non-negotiable. We maintain this for community health, safety, and clarity of mind.

Zero Tolerance

This includes "occasional" drinking, medical marijuana, and "just on weekends." If substances are part of your life, we're not the right community for you.

No Pets

We love animals, but shared living and pets don't mix well. Allergies, noise, damage, and conflicting care philosophies create friction. This applies to all pets.

Includes

Dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, rodents, fish tanks. Emotional support animals require prior approval with documentation.

Self-Directed

You manage your own schedule, responsibilities, and life. We don't wake you up, remind you of duties, or manage your time. Adults living as adults.

We Expect

You show up for commitments, handle your responsibilities without prompting, and communicate proactively when issues arise

Peaceful Coexistence

We leave each other alone. Friendly but not forced. No mandatory gatherings, no required socializing. Respect privacy and personal space above all.

The Culture

A nod in the kitchen, not a 30-minute chat. Shared meals are optional. Your time is yours.

4-Season Living

Real winters. Real summers. The Mid-Ohio Valley has distinct seasons. You'll experience snow, heat, humidity, and everything in between. Embrace it or reconsider.

Prepare For

Cold winters (20s-30s F), hot summers (80s-90s F), spring storms, beautiful falls. Seasonal wardrobes required.

The Full Handbook

Before applying, we ask that you read our complete Community Handbook. It covers everything from quiet hours to guest policies to maintenance responsibilities. If you can't commit to the standards, please don't apply.

Why So Detailed?

Because surprises are bad for everyone. We'd rather you know exactly what you're signing up for than have misunderstandings later. This protects you and protects the community.
