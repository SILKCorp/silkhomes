Where Intentional Living Meets Modern Hospitality

From diverse global communities to peaceful furnished homes across West Virginia and Ohio.

Inspired by Intentional Living

SILK Homes' founders grew up experiencing diverse intentional living communities across Asia and the USA. From a family-focused community in the Philippines to collaborative living in the Middle East, and a 100-acre forested property in West Virginia with 10 houses where everyone shared resources—these experiences shaped our understanding of meaningful community.

These formative experiences taught us that sharing resources and skills creates abundance, not scarcity. SILK Homes brings this wisdom into modern hospitality.

What We Provide Today

SILK Homes provides furnished housing and peaceful experiences for travelers seeking a blend of tranquil living and modern amenities. We specialize in serving those who want to experience intentional living—from traveling medical professionals to remote workers and conscious travelers.

Our homes offer more than accommodation; they provide spaces where guests can slow down, connect with productive gardens, and experience genuine hospitality in thoughtfully restored historical properties.

Each property features comfortable furnishings, modern conveniences, and victory gardens that guests can enjoy and learn from during their stay.

Our Properties

Our portfolio spans character-rich homes across West Virginia and Ohio, each thoughtfully restored and maintained. We steward historic properties from the 1800s, mid-century homes from the 1950s, and carefully selected modern dwellings—all designed to provide peaceful sanctuary for our guests.

We don't just provide lodging; we create spaces where travelers can experience intentional living, complete with productive gardens, historic charm, and modern comfort.

Where We're Going

SILK Homes is expanding our network of peaceful, furnished accommodations that serve travelers seeking meaningful experiences. We're developing more properties that blend historic preservation with sustainable living and modern comfort.

Our focus remains on providing exceptional furnished housing for those who value intentional living—whether you're a traveling healthcare professional needing a peaceful home base, or someone seeking to experience life in thoughtfully designed spaces.

We invite you to experience what it means to live intentionally, even temporarily, in homes designed for both comfort and connection to something greater.
