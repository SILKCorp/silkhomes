Where Intentional Living Meets Modern Hospitality

From rainforest porches and island homes to peaceful furnished places in the Mid-Ohio Valley.

Inspired by Intentional Living

SILK Homes' founders grew up experiencing diverse intentional living communities across Asia and the USA. From a family-focused community in the Philippines to collaborative living in the Middle East and a forested property in West Virginia where everyone shared resources, these experiences shaped our understanding of meaningful community.

These formative experiences taught us that sharing resources and skills creates abundance, not scarcity. SILK Homes brings this wisdom into modern hospitality.

What We Provide Today

SILK Homes provides furnished housing and peaceful experiences for travelers seeking a blend of tranquil living and modern amenities. We specialize in serving those who want to experience intentional living—from traveling medical professionals to remote workers and conscious travelers.

Our homes offer more than accommodation; they provide spaces where guests can slow down, connect with the rhythms of a property, and experience genuine hospitality in real furnished homes.

Each property is different. We show current room, garden, kitchen, and town context where we have an approved public derivative, and we keep the copy honest when coverage is still growing.

Our Properties

Our portfolio spans character-rich homes in the Mid-Ohio Valley and a wider family of places in Costa Rica, the Philippines, and the Virgin Islands. Each is maintained as a real place rather than a staged template: a room, a useful kitchen, a porch, a garden, and a town or landscape worth noticing.

No matter the climate or country, the promise is the same: quaint hospitality, peaceful living, and enough room for a guest to follow their own rhythm.

Where We're Going

SILK Homes is expanding our network of peaceful, furnished accommodations that serve travelers seeking meaningful experiences. We're developing more properties that blend historic preservation with sustainable living and modern comfort.

Our focus remains on providing exceptional furnished housing for those who value intentional living—whether you're a traveling healthcare professional needing a peaceful home base, or someone seeking to experience life in thoughtfully designed spaces.

We invite you to experience what it means to live intentionally, even temporarily, in homes designed for both comfort and connection to something greater.
