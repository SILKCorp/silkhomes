How to Join

Four pathways to become part of SILK Homes. Each serves different goals and commitment levels.

A clear way in

Find the right way to participate.

SILK Homes is a place to stay, a place to practice hospitality, and a place to build a more intentional working life. Start with the level of time, responsibility, and collaboration that fits you now.

The larger network

SILK Homes is part of SILK YACHT—Yoga, Arts, Cafe, Home, and Tech—within SILK Corp. The network connects people who want to learn, contribute, host, and eventually lead hospitality work of their own.

Stay & learn Work & contribute Host a program Build the next home

Start with a place

Every pathway begins with a real room and a real choice.

Some people come to rest. Some come to work. Some come ready to contribute. The room is the shared starting point; what you do with the time is personal.

Our pathways are intentionally different, but each asks for the same thing: arrive with curiosity, care for the home around you, and be honest about the kind of commitment you are ready to make.

Choose your starting point

Four pathways. One shared standard of care.

Each option has a different exchange of time, money, skill, and responsibility. Read for the fit—not just the title.

01 For guests and students

SILK Student

Stay, learn, and test the rhythm.

A short or long stay for experiencing intentional living before making a bigger commitment. Bring your curiosity, your own goals, and a willingness to participate at your own pace.

What comes with it

  • Private bedroom in a shared home
  • Access to available workshops and practices
  • Optional community meals and gatherings
  • Orientation from the resident crew

A good fit for

  • A reset, sabbatical, or transition
  • Learning homestead and hospitality skills
  • Trying a more communal way to live
Explore a stay
Most common
02 For capable contributors

Crew Member

Work with purpose and make a home run.

Bring practical skills to cooking, cleaning, gardening, housekeeping, or hospitality. This is a contribution-based arrangement for people who are already self-directed and dependable.

What we look for

  • YACHT balance: a foundation in wellness, creativity, hospitality, home care, and digital basics
  • Reliable, self-directed follow-through
  • A minimum 20-hour weekly commitment
  • References from previous work

What you can build

  • Private bedroom and practical home support
  • Hourly pay or housing credit, by arrangement
  • Experience across the full hospitality system
  • A possible route toward the franchisee track
See crew opportunities
03 For established guides

Retreat Facilitator

Bring a thoughtful program to a real home.

Yoga teachers, wellness practitioners, and workshop leaders can use SILK spaces as a grounded venue for small retreats, learning experiences, and restorative gatherings.

What we need from you

  • An established practice and relevant credentials
  • An existing student or client community
  • Insurance and current certifications
  • A clear program plan and track record

What we can support

  • Venue and lodging coordination
  • Meals and hospitality support by scope
  • Cross-network promotion when it fits
  • A transparent revenue-share conversation

04 For future operators

Franchisee Track

Learn the system, then lead a home of your own.

The long view: build a record of dependable work, learn the full hospitality model, and prepare to launch a SILK YACHT operation in another community.

A possible progression

  • Year 1: crew member—prove reliability
  • Year 2: team lead—support others
  • Year 3+: property manager—run operations
  • Certifications and business planning with SILK Corp

A good fit for

  • Entrepreneurs, women, and committed allies
  • Long-term builders who value earned trust
  • People ready to steward a place and a brand

What is "YACHT Balance"?

For Crew Members and Franchisees, we expect you to arrive with a foundation in all five YACHT elements. This isn't about being an expert - it's about being a well-rounded person who can contribute across the board.

Y

Yoga

A personal wellness practice. Yoga, meditation, movement. You take care of your body and mind.

A

Arts

Creative expression. Music, crafts, writing, design. You have an outlet and appreciate beauty.

C

Cafe

Hospitality skills. Cooking, serving, hosting. You can prepare a meal and make guests feel welcome.

H

Home

Domestic competence. Cleaning, organizing, basic repairs. You maintain spaces well.

T

Tech

Digital literacy. Email, spreadsheets, smart home basics. You're not intimidated by technology.

This is not a training program

We don't teach basic life skills. If you can't already cook a meal, clean a bathroom properly, or use a calendar app, we're not the right place to learn. Come capable, grow further.

The Application Process

1

Initial inquiry

Fill out our contact form with your background, which pathway interests you, and why SILK Homes appeals to you. Be honest and specific.

2

Handbook review

We send you our complete Community Handbook. Read it thoroughly. If anything is a dealbreaker, let us know. No judgment - better to know now.

3

Video interview

A 30-minute video call to discuss your background, goals, and fit. We'll ask about your YACHT balance and what you're looking for.

4

Trial stay

A 1-2 week paid visit to experience the community firsthand. You'll participate in daily life and meet current residents. Both sides evaluate fit.

5

Decision & onboarding

If it's a mutual fit, we discuss placement, start date, and specifics. You'll have a 90-day probationary period as we all adjust.
