Become a Part of SILK Homes

We're always looking for passionate individuals who share our love for homesteading, hospitality, and historical homes.

We have opportunities for local, part-time help with landscaping, housekeeping, and maintenance. For those looking for a deeper commitment, we offer opportunities to live on-site, manage a cluster of properties, and truly become an innkeeper in our community.

If you enjoy getting your hands dirty, making guests feel welcome, and contributing to a shared vision, you might be a perfect fit.

The work behind the welcome

Care happens in rooms, gardens, and the small systems between them.

A SILK Homes role is practical and place-aware: the work changes with each home, each season, and each guest.

Current Opportunities

Live-In Innkeeper & Garden Steward

Ravenswood, WV (Growing Home Network)
The Opportunity

We are seeking a dedicated individual or couple to help oversee a growing set of real homes in Ravenswood. This is a lifestyle role for someone who enjoys home care, gardening, and making people feel at home. Responsibilities include guest communication, coordinating cleanings, performing light maintenance, and supporting outdoor care as it fits each property.

Who We're Looking For

An experienced and self-motivated person with skills in guest services, basic home repair, and gardening. You are a problem-solver who is calm under pressure and genuinely loves creating a welcoming atmosphere. A passion for sustainability and community living is a must.

What We Offer

Role details, housing, and compensation are discussed directly based on the current opening. Become a core member of the SILK Homes work of care.

Part-Time Landscaper & Gardener

Parkersburg, WV
The Opportunity

Have a green thumb and a few hours to spare? We're looking for a local partner to help maintain lawns, entries, and garden areas across our Parkersburg homes. This is a practical role for someone who loves being outdoors and wants to contribute to well-kept homes without a live-in commitment.

What You'll Do

Mowing, trimming, weeding, planting, and harvesting based on the needs of the season. Work on your own schedule.

What We Offer

Competitive hourly pay and the satisfaction of making our corners of the world more beautiful, useful, and cared for.

Apply Now

Housekeeping & Staging Artisan

Marietta, OH
The Opportunity

We see housekeeping as a craft. It's the art of creating a space that feels clean, calm, and welcoming from the moment a guest walks in. We're looking for a detail-oriented individual to care for our Marietta homes between guest stays.

Who You Are

You find deep satisfaction in a perfectly made bed, a spotless kitchen, and a dust-free bookshelf. You are reliable, trustworthy, and work efficiently.

What We Offer

Flexible hours and excellent pay per job. You will be a trusted and respected member of our core team.

Apply Now

Community Builder & Jack-of-All-Trades

All Locations
The Opportunity

Don't see exactly what you're looking for above? We're always interested in meeting people who share our values and want to contribute to our community. Whether you have skills in carpentry, cooking, event planning, or something entirely different, we'd love to hear from you.

Who You Are

You're resourceful, reliable, and passionate about sustainable living. You might be between life transitions, looking for a change of pace, or seeking a community where you can grow personally and professionally.

What We Offer

Flexible arrangements based on your skills and our needs. This could range from project-based work to long-term community involvement.
