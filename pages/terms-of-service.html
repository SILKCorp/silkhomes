The promise in practice

Shared homes work best when expectations are visible.

These terms give the practical shape of our hospitality: care for the property, respect for other people, and a clear conversation when circumstances change.

1. Services

SILK Homes provides furnished accommodations and intentional living experiences in historic properties across West Virginia and Ohio. Our services include:

  • Short-term and extended stay accommodations
  • Intentional living community experiences
  • Employment opportunities with our hospitality crew
  • Community connection and educational programs

Booking Process: All reservations must be made through our official channels and confirmed in writing.

Payment: Payment terms and schedules will be specified at the time of booking confirmation.

Cancellations: Cancellation policies vary by property and length of stay. Specific terms will be provided with your booking confirmation.

As a guest, you agree to:
