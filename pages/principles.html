Our Guiding Principles

These aren't just aspirations—they're the living foundation of everything we do. From the way we restore weathered porches to the care we bring to every guest interaction, these principles guide our hands and our hearts.

🗝️

Hospitality is an Art of Welcome

For us, hospitality is never a transaction—it's a craft we practice daily. We create spaces where people feel genuinely welcomed, deeply safe, and truly at peace. It's in every freshly laundered pillowcase, every handwritten note, every thoughtful conversation. We are hosts in the truest, oldest sense of the word: keepers of the hearth who consider your comfort our honor.

In Practice:
  • Every guest receives personal, genuine attention
  • We maintain homes with the care we'd bring to our own
  • Our communication is warm, responsive, and always human
  • We notice the small things and anticipate needs quietly
🏛️

Honor the History, Build the Future

Our homes have weathered decades, some more than a century. We see ourselves as their temporary stewards, not their owners—we're simply this generation's caretakers in a long line of people who've called these walls home. We honor their past through careful, loving repair, and we ensure their future by making them sustainable and resilient for generations we'll never meet.

In Practice:
  • Thoughtful restoration using period-appropriate materials when we can find them
  • Preserving original details that tell the home's story
  • Blending old-world craftsmanship with modern sustainability
  • Listening to what these homes want to be, not forcing what we want them to become
🌱

Hands in the Soil, Roots in the Community

A home is so much more than four walls and a roof. It extends into the soil beneath our feet, the gardens and grounds that need attention, and the neighbors who share our street. Where a property has growing space, it can provide food, beauty, purpose, and a tangible connection to the rhythms of the earth.

In Practice:
  • Garden and grounds work shaped by each property
  • Growing, tending, and learning with the seasons
  • Sharing practical knowledge where it is useful
  • Teaching sustainable care without pretending a garden is finished
♻️

Resourcefulness is the New Luxury

We reject the disposable, the mass-produced, the soulless. True luxury, we've learned, lives in a well-made chair rescued from a thrift store, in the satisfaction of a skilled repair, in the creativity required to work with what we already have. This isn't just sustainable—though it is. It's how we fill our homes with character, history, and stories worth telling.

In Practice:
  • Furnishing homes with rescued, restored, and loved-again pieces
  • Choosing repair over replacement, always
  • Reimagining salvaged materials as beautiful, functional elements
  • Passing down traditional crafts and the skills that make self-sufficiency possible
🌙

Peaceful Living is Paramount

Our homes are intentional sanctuaries from the noise of the world. They're quiet havens for rest, reflection, and deep rejuvenation—especially vital for our guests, many of whom are healthcare workers giving everything they have to help others heal. We cultivate and fiercely protect a culture of mutual respect, where peace isn't just preferred, it's sacred.

In Practice:
  • Thoughtfully designed spaces that naturally encourage quiet
  • Clear, kind community agreements about shared peace
  • Careful selection of guests and crew who value tranquility
  • Choosing locations where silence is a natural amenity
  • Gardens and porches designed for meditation and stillness
  • Supporting true rest and work-life balance for our crew
⛵

SILK YACHT Integration: Holistic Living Spaces

We weave every aspect of the SILK YACHT philosophy into the fabric of our homes—creating balanced spaces that nurture the whole person. Yoga and movement. Arts and creativity. Cafe culture and nourishment. True hospitality. Modern connectivity. These aren't separate amenities we've added; they're integrated elements of how we believe people should live. Every corner of our properties is designed to support wellness, creativity, community, comfort, and the technology that connects us—all in equal measure, all in harmony.

YACHT Elements in Our Homes:
  • Yoga: Dedicated spaces for meditation, movement, and connecting breath to body—indoors and in our gardens
  • Arts: Creative corners filled with light, inspiration, and permission to make something beautiful
  • Cafe: Welcoming kitchens and dining spaces where cooking becomes community, enhanced by fresh herbs from our gardens
  • Homes: Restorative hospitality that feels like coming home—comfortable, thoughtful, and deeply human
  • Tech: Reliable connectivity and modern tools that support your work and life without dominating either
  • Balance: No single element overshadows another—each receives the attention it deserves in service of your whole life

Values become visible

A principle is only useful when it changes how a space is cared for.

Privacy, welcome, simplicity, and sustainability are not separate slogans. They show up in where a guest can work, how a shared room is maintained, and whether the garden is treated as decoration or resource.

We keep returning to the physical home because that is where the promise becomes testable: in the layout, the light, the tools, and the work required to make everyday life feel peaceful.

Living Our Values Daily

These aren't aspirational statements we hung on the wall—they're the living foundation of every decision we make. From the morning we greet a guest to the evening we harvest the last of the lettuce, our principles guide our hands, our words, and our work. This is how we actually live.
