Handyman Opportunities

Maintain and improve our unique properties where historic character meets modern sustainability and guest comfort.

🔧 Craftsmanship Meets Innovation

As a SILK Homes handyman, you'll work across real properties in West Virginia and Ohio, each with its own character, current condition, and maintenance needs.

This isn't typical property maintenance. You'll preserve historic features while integrating modern sustainable systems, solve unique challenges, and contribute to creating exceptional guest experiences in truly special properties.

The SILK Homes Difference
🏛️ Historic Preservation

Learn how to care for older homes and practical town properties while preserving their character and improving everyday comfort.

⚡ Modern Systems

Install and maintain energy-efficient systems, smart home technology, and sustainable solutions.

🌿 Sustainable Focus

Use eco-friendly materials and methods that align with our environmental values and guest expectations.

👥 Guest-Focused

Every repair and improvement enhances the guest experience while maintaining the property's unique character.

Key Responsibilities
Maintenance & Repair
  • Routine maintenance of plumbing, electrical, and HVAC systems
  • Preventive maintenance schedules and inspections
  • Quick response to guest-impacting issues
  • Seasonal maintenance and weatherization
  • Appliance and fixture maintenance and replacement
Improvement & Enhancement
  • Guest experience improvements and upgrades
  • Energy efficiency retrofits and installations
  • Historic feature restoration and preservation
  • Accessibility improvements and modifications
  • Smart home and automation system installation
Property Types & Unique Challenges
🏞️ Ravenswood Home Network

Scope: Ravenswood home network

  • Original hardwood floors and fixtures
  • Historic plumbing and electrical updates
  • Weather and moisture management
  • Distinct historic and everyday details
  • Garden and grounds integration
🏙️ Parkersburg Home Network

Scope: Parkersburg home network

  • Practical layouts and current furnishings
  • Original electrical and plumbing systems
  • Urban utility connections and code compliance
  • Efficient space utilization improvements
  • Modern convenience integrations
🎓 Marietta Home

Scope: Marietta home and grounds

  • Historic district compliance requirements
  • Current furnished-room care
  • Town-facing exterior maintenance
  • Seasonal property documentation
  • Garden and grounds maintenance
Specialized Skills & Projects
🔌 Electrical Systems

Upgrade historic wiring, install smart switches and outlets, LED lighting conversions, and electrical safety improvements.

🚰 Plumbing Updates

Modernize old plumbing, install efficient fixtures, address water pressure issues, and maintain period-appropriate appearances.

🌡️ HVAC & Efficiency

Install and maintain efficient heating/cooling systems that work with historic architecture and modern comfort needs.

🪟 Historic Restoration

Restore original windows, doors, and fixtures while improving their efficiency and functionality for modern use.

Requirements & Qualifications
Essential Skills
  • 3+ years general maintenance and repair experience
  • Basic knowledge of electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems
  • Experience with both hand tools and power tools
  • Problem-solving abilities for unique challenges
  • Reliable transportation to multiple properties
  • Physical ability for maintenance tasks
Preferred Qualifications
  • Experience with historic or older properties
  • Trade certifications (electrical, plumbing, HVAC)
  • Knowledge of sustainable building practices
  • Customer service and guest interaction skills
  • Experience with hospitality property maintenance
  • Interest in preserving historic character
Tools & Equipment

We provide comprehensive tool support for our handyman team:

Provided Equipment
  • Basic Tool Set: Quality hand tools and measuring equipment
  • Power Tools: Drill, saws, sanders, and specialty equipment
  • Testing Equipment: Electrical testers, moisture meters, diagnostic tools
  • Safety Equipment: Personal protective equipment and safety tools
Materials & Supplies
  • Quality Materials: Premium, sustainable materials for repairs and improvements
  • Specialty Items: Historic-appropriate fixtures and period materials
  • Emergency Supplies: Quick-response materials for urgent repairs
  • Vehicle Support: Work vehicle and transportation for materials
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience and specialized skills
  • Flexible scheduling with emergency response availability
  • Professional development in historic preservation and sustainable building
  • Tool and equipment allowance for personal tool preferences
  • Variety and challenge working on unique properties with interesting problems
  • Community connection with guests and crew members who appreciate craftsmanship
Ready to Join Our Maintenance Team?

Help us maintain and improve properties that tell stories, host communities, and create exceptional experiences for guests who value character, comfort, and sustainability.

Practical stewardship

Good maintenance protects the feeling of a home.

The best repair work is quiet. It keeps doors closing, rooms working, and small problems from becoming disruptions for the people living there.

A handyman at SILK Homes reads the building as a system: its layout, materials, seasons, and history. That context matters as much as the tool in hand.
