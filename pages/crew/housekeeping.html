Housekeeping Opportunities

Maintain the highest standards of cleanliness and care in our beautiful historic properties across West Virginia and Ohio.

🏡 Excellence in Historic Hospitality

As a SILK Homes housekeeping professional, you'll maintain the welcoming condition of real properties across four towns. Your work ensures that every guest experiences the comfort and care that make a furnished home feel ready.

This isn't typical hotel housekeeping. You'll care for homes with character, working with historic elements and modern amenities while maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness and presentation.

Our Housekeeping Standards

We follow Airbnb's professional cleaning standards and have developed additional protocols specific to our historic properties and community-focused mission.

📋 Professional Resource

Our housekeeping team uses the comprehensive Airbnb Cleaning Handbook as our foundation.

This resource provides detailed protocols for every aspect of short-term rental cleaning, from arrival preparation to departure standards.

What Makes SILK Homes Housekeeping Unique
🏛️ Historic Properties

Learn specialized care for older homes, current furnishings, shared rooms, and the ordinary details that make each property feel lived-in and ready.

🌿 Sustainable Practices

Use eco-friendly cleaning products and methods that align with our environmental values and guest expectations.

👥 Community Integration

Work closely with innkeepers and interact with guests who often become part of our extended community.

🎯 Attention to Detail

Every property tells a story—your work helps preserve and present that story beautifully to each guest.

Key Responsibilities
Guest Turnover Excellence
  • Complete cleaning between guest stays following Airbnb standards
  • Inspect and prepare all rooms for arriving guests
  • Manage laundry and linen service efficiently
  • Restock amenities and welcome materials
  • Document property condition and maintenance needs
Property Care & Maintenance
  • Deep cleaning and seasonal maintenance tasks
  • Care for historic elements and period fixtures
  • Coordinate with maintenance team on repairs
  • Maintain outdoor spaces and entryways
  • Inventory management and supply ordering
SILK Homes Cleaning Protocols

Beyond standard Airbnb requirements, we've developed additional protocols for our unique properties:

🪟 Historic Element Care

Specialized cleaning methods for original hardwood floors, stained glass, and period fixtures.

🌱 Garden Integration

Maintain garden-to-table cleanliness standards and fresh herb presentations for guests.

📚 Story Preservation

Careful handling of historical displays, books, and educational materials throughout properties.

♻️ Sustainability Focus

Use of eco-friendly products and waste reduction practices that align with guest values.

Requirements & Qualifications
Essential Skills
  • 2+ years professional housekeeping experience
  • Knowledge of hospitality cleaning standards
  • Attention to detail and quality standards
  • Physical ability for cleaning tasks
  • Reliable transportation to properties
  • Flexible scheduling for guest turnovers
Preferred Qualifications
  • Experience with historic or unique properties
  • Knowledge of eco-friendly cleaning products
  • Airbnb or short-term rental experience
  • Customer service experience
  • Interest in sustainable hospitality
  • Local knowledge of service providers
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience and property complexity
  • Flexible scheduling around guest check-ins and check-outs
  • Professional development in hospitality and historic property care
  • Community connection with guests and crew members
  • Beautiful work environment in unique, historic properties
  • Garden fresh perks including seasonal produce from property gardens
Training & Support

We provide comprehensive training to ensure success:

  • Airbnb Standards Training: Complete review of professional cleaning handbook and protocols
  • Property-Specific Training: Learn the unique features and care requirements of each property
  • Historic Property Care: Specialized training for maintaining period elements and fixtures
  • Sustainable Practices: Education on eco-friendly products and waste reduction methods
  • Guest Relations: Training on interacting with guests and representing SILK Homes values

The craft of welcome

Clean is only the beginning.

Housekeeping sets the emotional temperature of a room. The bed is made, the surfaces are clear, and the guest can understand how to settle in.

Our homes are furnished for real life, so staging is thoughtful rather than precious. The work is to preserve comfort, notice what needs attention, and leave each room ready for the next person.
