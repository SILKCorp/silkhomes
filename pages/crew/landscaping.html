Landscaping Opportunities

Shape the grounds around real homes and gardens across Mid-Ohio Valley towns.

🌿 Stewards of Beautiful Grounds

As a SILK Homes landscaping professional, you'll maintain the outdoor spaces that make our properties special. From victory gardens to historic grounds, you'll be responsible for creating and maintaining landscapes that reflect our commitment to beauty, sustainability, and connection to the land.

Each location offers unique challenges and opportunities, from riverfront properties to historic district gardens. Your expertise helps create the peaceful, productive environments our guests and crew members treasure.
