Landscaping Opportunities

Shape the grounds that frame our historic homes and community gardens across three beautiful regions.

🌿 Stewards of Beautiful Grounds

As a SILK Homes landscaping professional, you'll maintain the outdoor spaces that make our properties special. From victory gardens to historic grounds, you'll be responsible for creating and maintaining landscapes that reflect our commitment to beauty, sustainability, and connection to the land.

Each location offers unique challenges and opportunities, from riverfront properties to historic district gardens. Your expertise helps create the peaceful, productive environments our guests and crew members treasure.

Choose Your Location

Ravenswood, WV

Historic River Cottages

The Challenge

Four 1890s cottages with riverside gardens, heritage plantings, and our most extensive victory garden network.

Key Features
  • Riverfront landscaping considerations
  • Historic property restoration
  • Large productive garden spaces
  • Native plant preservation
View Ravenswood Position

Parkersburg, WV

Mid-Century Properties

The Challenge

1950s homes with established landscapes, urban garden integration, and community-focused outdoor spaces.

Key Features
  • Urban landscaping expertise
  • Mid-century design aesthetics
  • Efficient garden systems
  • Community space management
View Parkersburg Position

Marietta, OH

Historic Ohio Settlement

The Challenge

Ohio's first settlement featuring period-appropriate landscaping, heritage gardens, and historic preservation standards.

Key Features
  • Historic district compliance
  • Heritage plant varieties
  • Tourist-visible gardens
  • Educational landscaping
View Marietta Position

  • Experience with organic gardening methods
  • Equipment operation and maintenance

  • Passion for sustainable living
  • Attention to detail and aesthetics
  • Physical fitness for outdoor work
