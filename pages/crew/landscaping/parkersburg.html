Landscaping Position

Parkersburg, WV • Growing home network • Full-Time Position

Urban Garden Specialist

🏙️ Parkersburg, WV • 📍 Real homes and town lots

The Opportunity

Join our Parkersburg team maintaining real properties in an active river city. The work ranges from practical town lots and shared entries to gardens and outdoor spaces that are still finding their shape.

This is where furnished home care meets urban-scale gardening: useful systems, seasonal maintenance, and attention to the details residents and guests actually experience.

What You'll Manage
  • Distinct real homes: Different yards and layouts
  • Current public set: Kitchens, common rooms, and home details
  • Network growth: More property-specific coverage as it is approved
  • Urban vegetable and herb gardens
  • Front yard curb appeal maintenance
  • Efficient irrigation systems
  • Seasonal flower displays
Key Responsibilities
  • Town-Lot Garden Management: Use compact spaces, containers, and seasonal planting where they make sense
  • Grounds Care: Maintain welcoming landscaping that complements each home's actual character
  • Efficient Systems: Design and maintain water-efficient irrigation and low-maintenance plantings
  • Guest Convenience: Ensure gardens are productive but require minimal guest involvement unless requested
  • Urban Considerations: Work within city ordinances and neighborhood standards while promoting sustainable practices
  • Equipment Management: Maintain and operate urban-appropriate landscaping tools and equipment
  • Seasonal Adaptation: Plan for four-season growing using cold frames, season extension, and efficient crop rotation
What Makes This Position Special
🏗️ Different homes, one standard

Work with varied layouts and materials while keeping every property functional, welcoming, and well cared for.

🌆 Urban Innovation

Prove that sustainable living works in urban settings with efficient, beautiful garden systems.

⚡ Efficiency Focus

Our Parkersburg outdoor work prioritizes useful, maintainable spaces that support real residents and guests.

🏥 Healthcare Professionals

Many guests are traveling medical professionals who appreciate our convenient, low-maintenance approach.

Requirements
Essential Skills
  • 2+ years urban gardening or landscaping experience
  • Knowledge of container and intensive gardening
  • Experience with efficient irrigation systems
  • Ability to work within urban constraints
  • Reliable transportation to Parkersburg properties
Preferred Qualifications
  • Urban farming or gardening experience
  • Knowledge of season extension techniques
  • Experience with low-maintenance landscaping
  • Understanding of city gardening regulations
  • Passion for efficient, sustainable systems
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience
  • Flexible scheduling perfect for urban lifestyle
  • Fresh produce access from high-efficiency gardens
  • Professional development in urban sustainability
  • Community connection with healthcare and professional guests
  • Urban conveniences with easy access to all city amenities
Ready to Join Our Parkersburg Team?

Help us make sustainable living visible through practical, well-maintained home grounds and town-scale garden work.
