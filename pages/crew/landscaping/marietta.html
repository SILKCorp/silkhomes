Landscaping Position

Marietta, OH • Home and grounds • Full-Time Position

Heritage Garden Specialist

🏛️ Marietta, OH • 📍 Real home and grounds

The Opportunity

Join our Marietta team caring for a real furnished home in Ohio's first permanent settlement, where seasonal property care meets a living town context.

The work is deliberately specific: care for the grounds we actually have, document what is growing, and make improvements that support the home rather than invent a heritage story around it.

What You'll Manage
  • Real home: Furnished home and current grounds
  • Public archive: Room views now, more outdoor context as approved
  • Future coverage: Accurate imagery before expansive claims
  • Historic district-compliant landscaping
  • Heritage variety vegetable and herb gardens
  • Educational demonstration gardens
  • Period-appropriate ornamental plantings
Key Responsibilities
  • Garden Management: Grow useful plants and maintain outdoor spaces at the pace of the property
  • Historic Compliance: Ensure all landscaping meets historic district guidelines while promoting sustainability
  • Educational Component: Share practical knowledge about native plants, seasonal care, and the work of maintaining a garden
  • Town Context: Maintain welcoming grounds that respect the surrounding neighborhood
  • Collaboration with home stewards: Integrate garden activities with guest experiences where appropriate
  • Seasonal Programming: Design four-season interest with historical accuracy and modern sustainability
  • Research & Documentation: Research historical plantings and maintain records of heritage varieties
What Makes This Position Special
📚 Living History

Create gardens that tell the story of Ohio's first settlement and American agricultural heritage.

🎓 Educational Impact

Your work makes sustainable practice visible through ordinary, well-cared-for spaces.

🏆 Recognition

The Marietta garden story is still being documented; the priority is accurate care and honest public coverage.

🤝 Community Connection

Work with home stewards and local partners on practical, place-aware garden care.

Requirements
Essential Skills
  • 3+ years landscaping or horticulture experience
  • Knowledge of heritage plants and heirloom varieties
  • Understanding of historic preservation guidelines
  • Educational or public-facing experience preferred
  • Reliable transportation to Marietta properties
Preferred Qualifications
  • Experience with heritage/historical gardening
  • Knowledge of Ohio River Valley native plants
  • Historic district or preservation experience
  • Interest in educational programming
  • Passion for historical research and documentation
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience
  • Flexible scheduling accommodating tourist seasons
  • Garden access shaped by current property needs
  • Educational opportunities through practical, place-aware work
  • Accurate documentation as the public archive grows
  • Cultural enrichment in Ohio's most historic community
Ready to Join Our Marietta Team?

Help us maintain a real home and its grounds with care, accuracy, and respect for the living town around it.
