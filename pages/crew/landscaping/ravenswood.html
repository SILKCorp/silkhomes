Landscaping Position

Ravenswood, WV • Growing home network • Full-Time Position

Grounds & Garden Specialist

🏞️ Ravenswood, WV • 📍 Real homes and grounds

The Opportunity

Join our team in maintaining real properties connected through one Ravenswood network. You'll help care for the grounds, gardens, paths, and outdoor details that make a furnished home feel held.

This is practical horticulture in a real property network: seasonal work, changing conditions, and gardens that grow through repeated attention rather than instant perfection.

What You'll Manage
  • Distinct real homes: Different yards and stages of development
  • Shared grounds: Paths, entries, and gathering edges
  • Garden areas: Productive, ornamental, and in progress
  • Town network: Care that respects each home's character
  • Shared pathways and common areas
  • River access and waterfront maintenance
  • Heritage plant preservation
  • Seasonal flower displays
Key Responsibilities
  • Garden Management: Plan, plant, maintain, and document vegetable, herb, and ornamental areas
  • Grounds Maintenance: Mowing, pruning, weeding, and general upkeep of all outdoor spaces
  • Riverfront Care: Special attention to plants and erosion control along the Ohio River
  • Guest Integration: Create opportunities for guests to participate in garden activities
  • Seasonal Planning: Four-season maintenance including winter preparation and spring restoration
  • Equipment Management: Maintain and operate landscaping tools and equipment
  • Native Plant Focus: Emphasize plants native to the Ohio River Valley
What Makes This Position Special
🏛️ Historic Preservation

Work with plantings that respect each property's character while meeting practical sustainability goals.

🌊 Riverfront Beauty

Manage landscapes that transition beautifully from cottage gardens to natural riverbank.

🍅 Productive Gardens

Our Ravenswood garden work is part of the wider home network and grows according to each property's space, light, and current capacity.

👥 Community Connection

Work closely with home stewards and engage with guests who want to notice or participate in the care of a property.

Requirements
Essential Skills
  • 2+ years landscaping or gardening experience
  • Knowledge of organic/sustainable practices
  • Physical ability for outdoor labor
  • Basic equipment operation skills
  • Reliable transportation to Ravenswood
Preferred Qualifications
  • Experience with heritage/native plants
  • Vegetable garden management
  • River/waterfront landscaping knowledge
  • Historic property experience
  • Passion for intentional living
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience
  • Flexible scheduling with seasonal variations
  • Access to fresh produce from the gardens you maintain
  • Community connection with like-minded individuals
  • Professional development in sustainable landscaping
  • Beautiful work environment in a historic riverside setting
Ready to Join Our Ravenswood Team?

Help us maintain one of the most beautiful properties in the Ohio River Valley while contributing to our community's vision of sustainable, intentional living.

Ravenswood, West Virginia

Care for the edge between home and landscape.

Ravenswood rewards a steward who can see the whole scene: the house, the yard, the seasonal changes, and the quiet ways people move through them.

The work ranges from useful garden care to keeping outdoor transitions calm and cared for. It is a role for someone who notices what guests and residents need before it becomes a problem.
