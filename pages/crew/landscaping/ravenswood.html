Landscaping Position

Ravenswood, WV • Historic Riverfront Cottages • Full-Time Position

Grounds & Garden Specialist

🏞️ Ravenswood, WV • 📍 Four Historic 1890s Cottages

The Opportunity

Join our team in maintaining the crown jewel of SILK Homes—four magnificent 1890s cottages along the Ohio River. You'll be responsible for the grounds and gardens that make Ravenswood our most beautiful and productive location.

This is where history meets horticulture. You'll work with heritage plants, manage our largest victory garden network, and maintain landscapes that guests remember long after they leave.

What You'll Manage
  • The Riverside Cottage: Herb garden and river views
  • The Garden House: Our most productive victory garden
  • The Craftsman: Ornamental and privacy landscaping
  • Innkeeper's Residence: Community gathering spaces
  • Shared pathways and common areas
  • River access and waterfront maintenance
  • Heritage plant preservation
  • Seasonal flower displays
Key Responsibilities
  • Victory Garden Management: Plan, plant, maintain, and harvest our extensive vegetable and herb gardens
  • Grounds Maintenance: Mowing, pruning, weeding, and general upkeep of all outdoor spaces
  • Riverfront Care: Special attention to plants and erosion control along the Ohio River
  • Guest Integration: Create opportunities for guests to participate in garden activities
  • Seasonal Planning: Four-season maintenance including winter preparation and spring restoration
  • Equipment Management: Maintain and operate landscaping tools and equipment
  • Native Plant Focus: Emphasize plants native to the Ohio River Valley
What Makes This Position Special
🏛️ Historic Preservation

Work with plantings that complement 1890s architecture while meeting modern sustainability goals.

🌊 Riverfront Beauty

Manage landscapes that transition beautifully from cottage gardens to natural riverbank.

🍅 Productive Gardens

Our Ravenswood victory gardens produce food for guests and contribute to the local community.

👥 Community Connection

Work closely with our live-in innkeeper and engage with guests who love to garden.

Requirements
Essential Skills
  • 2+ years landscaping or gardening experience
  • Knowledge of organic/sustainable practices
  • Physical ability for outdoor labor
  • Basic equipment operation skills
  • Reliable transportation to Ravenswood
Preferred Qualifications
  • Experience with heritage/native plants
  • Vegetable garden management
  • River/waterfront landscaping knowledge
  • Historic property experience
  • Passion for intentional living
Compensation & Benefits
  • Competitive hourly wage based on experience
  • Flexible scheduling with seasonal variations
  • Access to fresh produce from the gardens you maintain
  • Community connection with like-minded individuals
  • Professional development in sustainable landscaping
  • Beautiful work environment in a historic riverside setting
Ready to Join Our Ravenswood Team?

Help us maintain one of the most beautiful properties in the Ohio River Valley while contributing to our community's vision of sustainable, intentional living.
