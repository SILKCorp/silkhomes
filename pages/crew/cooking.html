    • Cooking & Cafe Opportunities

    Join our network of culinary artists creating homemade goods for our cafes and community, with delivery and pickup services.

    ☕ Community Cafes & Culinary Partnership

    SILK Homes operates community cafes in select properties, offering guests and locals access to wholesome, homemade food that reflects our values of sustainability, health, and community connection.

    We're looking for passionate culinary artists to create delicious, healthy food that meets our dietary guidelines while serving our community through cafe sales, special orders, and catering for guests and events.

    Our Culinary Philosophy
    🌱 Garden-to-Table

    We prioritize ingredients from our own victory gardens and local farms, creating seasonal menus that reflect what's growing in our community.

    🍽️ Wholesome & Homemade

    Every item is prepared from scratch using traditional techniques and high-quality, minimally processed ingredients.

    🤝 Community Focused

    Our cafes serve as gathering spaces where guests and locals can connect over shared meals and experiences.

    🌟 Health Conscious

    We follow specific dietary guidelines to ensure our food supports health and wellness for all community members.

    Essential Dietary Guidelines

    All food prepared for SILK Homes cafes must meet these requirements:

    • 🚫 No Pork: No pork products or pork-derived ingredients
    • 🦐 No Shellfish: No crustaceans, mollusks, or shellfish of any kind
    • 🥗 Minimally Processed: Whole food ingredients, limited preservatives
    • 🌿 Fresh & Natural: Emphasize seasonal, local, and organic when possible
    • ⚖️ Balance: Nutritious options that support overall wellness
    • 🏷️ Transparency: Clear ingredient labeling for all dietary needs
    Opportunities Available
    🏪 Cafe Kitchen Artist

    Type: Regular Partnership

    Work directly in our cafe kitchens to prepare daily offerings, seasonal specials, and guest breakfast items. Perfect for those who enjoy consistent, community-focused cooking.

    • Daily baked goods and breakfast items
    • Lunch specials using garden produce
    • Seasonal menu development
    • Direct guest interaction
    🚚 Delivery & Pickup Service

    Type: Flexible Partnership

    Prepare homemade goods from your own kitchen for pickup or delivery to our properties. Ideal for home bakers and cooks who want flexibility.

    • Weekly or monthly delivery schedules
    • Specialty items and baked goods
    • Custom orders for events
    • Work from your own kitchen
    🎂 Special Events Catering

    Type: Project-Based

    Create custom menus for guest celebrations, community events, and special gatherings hosted at our properties.

    • Wedding and celebration catering
    • Community dinner events
    • Holiday and seasonal celebrations
    • Corporate retreat meals
    🌾 Farm-to-Table Specialist

    Type: Seasonal Partnership

    Develop seasonal menus that showcase our garden harvest and local farm partnerships, creating educational dining experiences.

    • Victory garden harvest utilization
    • Preservation and canning projects
    • Educational cooking demonstrations
    • Local farmer collaborations
    Food Safety & Professional Standards

    All culinary artists working with SILK Homes must meet professional food safety standards:

    Required Certifications
    • Food Handler's License: Current certification required
    • Safe Food Handling: Knowledge of temperature controls and storage
    • Sanitation Protocols: Proper cleaning and sanitizing procedures
    • Allergen Awareness: Understanding of allergen management
    Professional Practices
    • Kitchen Standards: Commercial-level cleanliness and organization
    • Documentation: Proper labeling, dating, and inventory tracking
    • Quality Control: Consistent preparation and presentation
    • Health Compliance: Adherence to local health department regulations
    Popular Menu Categories

    Our community especially enjoys these types of homemade offerings:

    🥐 Breakfast & Baked Goods

    Fresh breads, pastries, granola, and healthy breakfast options using garden herbs and seasonal fruits.

    🥗 Garden-Fresh Lunch

    Soups, salads, and light meals featuring produce from our victory gardens and local farms.

    🍯 Preserved & Specialty

    Jams, pickles, herb blends, and preserved goods that showcase seasonal harvests.

    🌿 Wellness Focused

    Herbal teas, tonics, and health-conscious treats that support our community's wellness goals.

    Partnership Benefits
    • Flexible Scheduling: Work arrangements that fit your lifestyle and other commitments
    • Creative Freedom: Develop menus and specialties that showcase your culinary talents
    • Community Impact: Feed and nourish guests and locals who value wholesome, homemade food
    • Garden Access: Use fresh herbs and produce from our victory gardens in your cooking
    • Steady Income: Regular orders and seasonal contracts provide dependable revenue
    • Professional Growth: Develop your culinary business in a supportive community environment
