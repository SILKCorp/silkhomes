Live-In Opportunities

Experience the full SILK YACHT lifestyle while contributing to our community operations through immersive live-in positions.

🏡 The Full SILK YACHT Experience

Our live-in opportunities offer the chance to experience intentional community living while contributing your skills to SILK Homes operations. These positions provide housing, community connection, and involvement in the full spectrum of SILK YACHT activities.

This is more than a job—it's a lifestyle choice. Live-in positions allow you to fully integrate into our community values while gaining experience across multiple areas of sustainable hospitality and community building.

What is SILK YACHT?

SILK YACHT is our integrated wellness ecosystem that encompasses all aspects of intentional living:

🧘 Yoga & Wellness

Physical and mental wellness practices, meditation, and holistic health approaches.

🎨 Arts & Culture

Creative expression, cultural events, and artistic community engagement.

☕ Cafe & Community

Culinary arts, community dining, and food as a connector for relationships.

🏡 Homes & Hospitality

Sustainable living spaces, hospitality excellence, and community building.

⚡ Tech (Coming Soon)

Technology solutions that support community, sustainability, and connection.

Live-In Position Types
🏠 Innkeeper Positions

Location: Ravenswood & Marietta

Live on-site as the primary host and operations manager for a property cluster, welcoming guests and managing daily operations.

  • Private cottage or apartment included
  • Guest relations and hospitality
  • Property oversight and coordination
  • Community event facilitation
🌱 Community Steward

Location: All Properties

Live in shared community housing while contributing to multiple aspects of property maintenance, gardens, and guest services.

  • Shared living arrangements
  • Rotation through different roles
  • Skill development across SILK YACHT
  • Community building focus
🎓 Learning Residency

Duration: 3-12 months

Immersive experience designed for those wanting to learn sustainable living, hospitality, and community building skills.

  • Structured learning program
  • Mentorship and training
  • Experience across all YACHT components
  • Personal development focus
🚀 Innovation Fellow

Focus: Special Projects

Lead specific initiatives in sustainability, technology, community building, or hospitality innovation while living in community.

  • Project-based residence
  • Innovation and experimentation
  • Leadership development
  • Professional portfolio building

Living Arrangements & Compensation
What's Included
  • Housing: Private or shared living space on property
  • Utilities: All utilities, internet, and property amenities
  • Food Access: Garden produce and community meal participation
  • Learning: Training and skill development opportunities
  • Community: Full integration into SILK YACHT activities
Compensation Structure
  • Stipend: Monthly living allowance based on role and experience
  • Professional Development: Training, certifications, and skill-building
  • Network Access: Connections within intentional living community
  • Experience Value: Portfolio-building in hospitality and sustainability
  • Future Opportunities: Potential for permanent positions or partnerships
Ideal Candidates
Personal Qualities
  • Passion for intentional living and community
  • Flexibility and adaptability
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Initiative and self-motivation
  • Commitment to sustainable practices
  • Openness to learning and growth
Experience & Skills
  • Customer service or hospitality background preferred
  • Any combination of: gardening, cooking, maintenance, arts
  • Interest in historic preservation or sustainability
  • Leadership or community organizing experience
  • Entrepreneurial mindset and problem-solving abilities
  • Cultural awareness and inclusive practices
Application Process

Our live-in positions require a thoughtful matching process to ensure mutual fit:

  1. Initial Application: Share your background, interests, and what draws you to intentional living
  2. Video Interview: Discuss your goals, expectations, and potential contributions
  3. Visit Weekend: Spend 2-3 days experiencing life at one of our properties
  4. Community Integration: Meet current residents and participate in community activities
  5. Mutual Decision: Both parties decide if the arrangement is a good fit
