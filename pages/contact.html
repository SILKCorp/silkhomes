Frequently Asked Questions

SILK Homes is rooted in community values and sustainable living. Our real homes include furnished rooms, shared spaces, gardens and grounds in progress, and a commitment to peaceful, intentional living. We're not just providing accommodation—we're offering a more grounded way to stay.

While relevant experience is helpful, we value attitude and alignment with our principles over credentials. We're looking for people who are passionate about community living, sustainability, and hospitality. We provide training and support for the right candidates.

We typically respond to inquiries within 48-72 hours. For crew applications, we may take up to a week to review and respond, as we carefully consider each application. If you haven't heard back within that timeframe, please feel free to follow up.
