FAQs

Common Inquiries About SILK Homes

Start here for the practical questions. Because every SILK home has its own history, layout, and town context, we confirm home-specific details directly before you arrive.

Arrival and departure windows depend on the home, the previous stay, and the crew’s preparation schedule. We provide the confirmed window and access instructions with your stay details; ask early if your travel requires flexibility.
Pets are considered home by home because of the property, other residents, allergies, and care requirements. Tell us about your animal before booking so we can give you a clear answer rather than a generic promise.
There is no single minimum that applies to every SILK home. Short stays, mid-term arrangements, and crew or participation stays each have different practical needs. Share your dates and purpose and we will outline what fits.
Homes are furnished for everyday living, generally with a private bedroom, shared living and kitchen areas, connectivity, linens, and home-specific essentials. We identify exactly what is included in the availability note for the property you are considering.
We do not rely on artificial packages or invented discounts. We talk through the kind of stay you need—short, shared, mid-term, or participation-based— and explain the current terms honestly.
